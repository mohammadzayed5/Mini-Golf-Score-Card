# Contact & Support

Mini Golf Score Tracker is made by one person, and every email comes straight to me. There is no ticket system and no bot in the middle.

## Email

[support@minigolfscoretracker.com](mailto:support@minigolfscoretracker.com)

If that link does not open anything, copy the address by hand:

`support@minigolfscoretracker.com`

## Before you email

A lot of questions already have answers:

- [Frequently asked questions](/#faq) covers cost, accounts, offline play, and player limits
- [The guides](/guides) cover rules, scoring, party planning, and building a backyard course
- [Privacy policy](/privacy) covers what the app stores and what it does not

## What to include in a bug report

I can usually fix something on the first reply if the email has:

1. iPhone or browser, and which version
2. What you tapped, in order
3. What you expected, and what happened instead
4. A screenshot if the screen looked wrong

Without those I have to write back and ask, which costs us both a day.

## Common requests

**Delete my account and everything in it.** You can do this yourself in the app: Account Settings, then Delete Account. If you would rather I do it, email from any address and say which username to remove.

**Take someone's name out.** The app stores player names exactly as they are typed in, and those are often other people, including kids. If a name of yours or your child's is in someone's scorecard and you want it gone, email me and I will delete it. You do not need an account to ask.

**Report a bug or ask for a feature.** Both very welcome. Feature requests genuinely do get built.

**Privacy or data questions.** Start with the [privacy policy](/privacy). If it does not answer your question, email me and I will.

## Response time

I aim to reply within a few days. It is one person and not a support desk, so weekends and holidays run slower. Every email does get read.
